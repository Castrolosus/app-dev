## About Squid Game
Squid Game is a korean survival series where people with financial problems join some children's games to win a huge amount of money. Sounds fun until you realize that losing means dying.

the series shows how desperate people can become when they have no other choice. life is already hard enough without having to play red light green light for money.


## Characters
- Seong Gi-hun - just a guy trying to fix his life.
- Cho Sang-woo - smart but makes some really bad decisions.
- Kang Sae-byeok - just wants a better life for herself and her family.


## Games
1. Red Light, Green Light
2. Dalgona Candy
3. Tug of War
4. Marbles
5. Glass Bridge
6. Squid Game


## Why I Like It
- the story is interesting.
- the games are creative.
- the plot twists are unexpected.
- the characters actually have good backstories.
- it shows how people act when money and survival are involved.
